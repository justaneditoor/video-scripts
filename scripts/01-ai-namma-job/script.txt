*“Humans repetitive-ah pannura work-ah, machine moolama faster-ah, easier-ah panna mudiyuma?”*

*“Why am I still doing the same repetitive work for the last one or two years?”*

Because today, it's not just about how many years you've worked.
It's about:
*“What new value are you bringing?”*
